The most agile platform in the fleet. Small enough for close-quarters and urban deployment, the 5-inch interceptor trades raw range for unmatched maneuverability and near-instant response time, piloted over a low-latency analog FPV video link.

Conceptual Platform Spec

A high-speed 5-inch platform, built for velocity.

This is a conceptual specification outline for a high-performance 5-inch-class airframe targeting speeds of approximately 250 km/h — describing platform-level requirements and material considerations, not a build guide.

Airframe & Structure

A lightweight, high-strength composite frame engineered to absorb sustained high-speed loads without adding unnecessary mass.

Aerodynamic Construction

Streamlined arm and body profiling that reduces drag and turbulence at velocity, keeping the airframe stable under high-speed airflow.

Durable Propellers

High-RPM-rated propellers built to hold their profile and balance under sustained high-speed rotation.

Flight-Control Electronics

A high-sample-rate flight controller and sensor stack suited to the rapid attitude corrections a high-speed platform demands.

High-Current Power System

A power distribution system sized for sustained high-current draw, with short, low-resistance paths from battery to motor.

Wiring & Connectors

Lightweight, low-resistance wiring and connectors rated for high continuous current with minimal added weight.

Vibration-Resistant Mounting

Isolated, vibration-dampened mounting for flight-control electronics to preserve sensor accuracy at high RPM.

Thermal Management

Adequate passive airflow paths around power electronics to manage heat under sustained high-output operation.

Target Performance

Conceptually positioned for speeds of approximately 250 km/h in a 5-inch-class airframe, prioritizing a high thrust-to-weight, low-drag configuration.

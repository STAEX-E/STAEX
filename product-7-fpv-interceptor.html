Built for velocity. The 7-inch interceptor reaches up to 300 km/h on a 6S 2,500 mAh pack, closing distance fast to neutralize aerial threats before they reach protected airspace. Non-jammable, non-ISM band telemetry keeps it linked under electronic countermeasures, carrying a 200 g payload across roughly 13 minutes of high-speed flight.
